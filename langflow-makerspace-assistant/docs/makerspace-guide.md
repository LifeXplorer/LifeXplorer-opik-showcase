# Quillmere Makerspace: Member Guide (fictional)

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 10:00 to 21:00
- Saturday: 09:00 to 18:00
- Sunday: closed for maintenance, except the first Sunday of each month (12:00 to 16:00, open build day)
- Staff-supervised hours (required for the laser cutter and the metal shop): Tuesday to Saturday, 14:00 to 20:00

## Booking tools
Book every machine through the front-desk kiosk or the member portal. Slots are 30 minutes; the maximum is 4 hours per machine per day.

| Tool | Slot rules | Cost per hour |
|---|---|---|
| 3D printers (6x Prusa-style FDM) | Bookable without supervision once you hold a Yellow badge | 2 credits |
| Laser cutter (80 W CO2, "Beam Me Up") | Supervised hours only, Orange badge | 6 credits |
| CNC router ("Gordon") | Orange badge, 2-hour minimum | 8 credits |
| Soldering benches (8) | Walk-in, no booking needed | free |
| Metal shop (welder, bandsaw) | Supervised hours only, Red badge | 10 credits |

Cancel at least 2 hours ahead or the credits are not refunded. A no-show twice in one month pauses your booking rights for 7 days.

## Badges
- Yellow: complete the 45-minute Orientation video plus a floor walk-through with any staff member. Free.
- Orange: Yellow badge plus the 2-hour hands-on class for the machine. 15 credits per class.
- Red: Orange badge plus 5 logged supervised hours plus a sign-off from the shop lead.

## Safety rules
1. Closed-toe shoes in every workshop zone. No exceptions, even for a quick look.
2. Tie back long hair and remove dangling jewellery or lanyards near rotating tools (CNC router, bandsaw).
3. Safety glasses are mandatory at the CNC router, bandsaw, welder and when sanding. Welding also needs a shade 10 helmet from the wall rack.
4. Never leave the laser cutter running unattended, and never cut PVC, vinyl or anything containing chlorine: it releases toxic gas. Approved materials are on the laminated list by the machine.
5. Do not use a machine you are not badged for, and never lend your badge.
6. Report every injury, however small, at the front desk before you leave. First-aid kits hang by each door; the eyewash station is next to the metal shop.
7. In a fire alarm, stop work, power down machines with the red mushroom button, and meet at the bike racks.
8. Under-16s must be accompanied by a badged adult and may not use the laser cutter, CNC router or metal shop.

## Contact
Questions the guide does not answer go to the front desk (desk@quillmere.example). For anything not covered here, say so and point the member to the desk.
