# Club Sites, Events and Membership

## Marrow Ridge dark-sky site

Marrow Ridge is a fenced field forty minutes north of Brightwater, with a warm-up hut, a wind fence and eight concrete pier pads with 12-volt power points. Gate codes are changed every quarter and sent by email to paid-up members only. Cars must park at the lower lot and use red-filtered side lights on the access track.

## Star party calendar

The club hosts three star parties a year: the Spring Messier Marathon in March, the Perseid Picnic in August and the Winter Nebula Night in December. The Perseid Picnic is the only one open to non-members, and guests must be accompanied by a member.

## Membership fees

Adult membership costs 45 pounds a year, student membership costs 20 pounds and a family membership covering two adults and any children costs 70 pounds. Fees fall due on 1 January and cover loaner telescope access and site gate codes.

## Borrowing a loaner telescope

Members may borrow a loaner telescope for up to two weeks, with one extension if nobody has joined the waiting list. Borrowers sign the equipment log and pay a refundable 25 pound deposit. Anything damaged in the loan period is repaired from the club's equipment fund.
