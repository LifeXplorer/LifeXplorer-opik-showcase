# Bottling and carbonation at Copperkettle

## How much priming sugar do I add for a 5-gallon batch?
Use 4.5 ounces (127 grams) of corn sugar, dissolved in two cups of boiled water
and cooled, for a standard ale at about 2.4 volumes of CO2. Use 3.5 ounces for a
British-style ale and 5.5 ounces for a wheat beer. Weigh the sugar; scoops vary.

## How long should bottles condition?
Store bottles at 68-72 F (20-22 C) for two weeks. Chill one bottle for a day and
open it. If it is flat, give the batch another week. Strong beers can need
four weeks. Once carbonated, move bottles to a cool, dark place.

## What bottles are safe to use?
Use only brown or green glass bottles meant for beer, with a standard crown cap.
Never reuse bottles with a twist-off cap, and never use thin-walled
bottles; they cannot hold the pressure. Discard any bottle with chips or cracks.

## Can I use maple syrup instead of corn sugar for priming?
Yes, at about 7 ounces (200 mL) of pure maple syrup per 5 gallons. Maple syrup is
only partly fermentable, so expect slightly lower carbonation and a very subtle flavor.
