# Sanitation at Copperkettle

## What sanitizer do we use?
Copperkettle brewers use a no-rinse acid sanitizer at 1 ounce per 5 gallons of
water, with a contact time of two minutes. Foam is harmless, so do not rinse it
away. Bleach is not allowed in the brewhouse because chlorophenol off-flavors
are hard to remove from plastic fermenters.

## Do I need to sanitize the grain bag and the mash tun?
No. Everything that touches the wort before the boil will be sanitized by the
boil itself. Sanitation matters for anything that touches the wort after it has
cooled: fermenter, lid, airlock, spoon, thermometer, and the tubing you use to transfer.

## How do I know if my batch is infected?
Look for a white film that is not a thin krausen ring, a sour or vinegar smell,
or ropy, slimy beer. A pellicle on top of a beer that smells fine is usually
harmless, but if a batch smells of vinegar or rotten fruit, pour it out. Do not
bottle an infected beer: it can over-carbonate and burst the bottles.
